# Walkthrough: Landing Page, Login, Register, Pricing & Service Redesigned

I have completed all the requested UI design changes: remodeling the landing page, login page, register page, pricing page, and service page to match the designs.

---

## 1. Landing Page Remodeled
- **Scroll Pop-Up Animation**: Added a spring/elastic ease animation (`cubic-bezier(0.34, 1.56, 0.64, 1)`) for all sections and testimonials when scrolling. Elements scale down and offset slightly, then pop up with a premium spring bounce as they enter the viewport.
- **Corrected Design Assets**:
  - Swapped Features mockup to correctly display the laptop and phone mockup (`images/landing page/Group 15.png`).
  - Swapped Creator Showcase to correctly display the creator holding the laptop (`images/landing page/Group 17.png` which has the floating tags pre-rendered on the asset).

---

## 2. Redesigned Login Page
- **Split-Screen Layout**:
  - **Left Side (Form Container)**: Styled with a clean white background.
    - Centered KLiNKAN logo at the top.
    - Title **"Selamat Datang Kembali"** and subtitle **"Masuk ke Linkan anda"**.
    - Fully rounded capsule-shaped input fields with thin borders.
    - Capsule-shaped "Masuk" button with a white background, thin border, and hover effect.
    - Google Login button with Google's logo.
  - **Right Side (Mockup Showcase)**: Styled with a solid brand orange background.
    - Centered the tilted phone mockup (`images/login/Group 18.png`) displaying the Linkan profile and floating tags, complete with a mouse-follow float animation.

---

## 3. Redesigned Register Page
- **Split-Screen Layout**:
  - **Left Side (Form Container)**: Styled with a clean white background.
    - Title **"Buat Akun Anda"**.
    - Vertically stacked form fields with labels above the capsule input fields: *Full Name*, *User Name*, *Email*, *Password*, and *Confirm Password*.
    - Solid black capsule submit button labeled **"Create Account"**.
    - Link to sign in: *"Already have an account? Sign in"*.
  - **Right Side (Welcome Panel)**: Styled with a solid brand blue background (`#0A60D4`).
    - Centered title **"Selamat datang di"** at the top.
    - Floating Linkan favicon logo with shadow.
    - Brand title **"Linkan"**.
    - Description text: *"Join the vibrant creators economy. set up your fluid microsited, sell digital products dan receive donatios instantly"*.
  - **Responsiveness**: The blue welcome panel is hidden on mobile screens (`max-width: 768px`) to fit forms cleanly on smaller devices.

---

## 4. Redesigned Pricing Page
- **Solid Orange Background**: The entire background is styled with a solid brand orange (`#EE8025`).
- **Capsule Header & Footer**: Added the white capsule navbar (with the **Pricing** link active and highlighted in orange, and **Sign Up** styled as a black capsule button) and capsule footer to match the design.
- **Centered Pricing Card**:
  - Encased in a white card with a thick **5px royal blue border** (`#0062E6`).
  - Top gray banner header containing **"Free"** text.
  - Large **"Rp 0 Forever"** pricing label.
  - Full-width blue capsule **"Get Started"** button.
  - Detailed feature list labeled **"Everything in Free :"** using custom black checkmark circles (`.check-circle` with inline SVGs).

---

## 5. Redesigned Service Page
- **Solid Blue Background**: The entire background is styled with a solid brand blue (`#0A60D4`).
- **Capsule Header & Footer**: Added the white capsule navbar (with the **Service** link active and highlighted in orange, and **Sign Up** styled as a black capsule button) and capsule footer.
- **Split-Screen Main Content**:
  - **Left Side (Text Side)**: Contains the main title **"Digital marketing for your business"** and description, followed by a white capsule **"Get Service"** button with blue text.
  - **Right Side (Mockup Side)**: Centers the vertical blue-themed phone mockup (`images/landing page/Service.png`) displaying the Linkan profile page, complete with a mouse-follow translation floating animation.
- **Responsiveness**: Shifts to a single centered vertical column on mobile screens (`max-width: 768px`) to look premium and readable on smaller viewports.
